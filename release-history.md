## 2026-11
- Published pages: 102
- Immutable edition root: `/editions/2026-11/`
- Movable latest pointer: `/latest/`
- Patch releases: `/editions/2026-11-patch-N/`
- Product name: DisclosureWorks Sourcebook. New address: sourcebook.disclosureworks.ai; Earlier addresses redirect permanently (301) to the same path. Page IDs and machine keys are unchanged.
- Released: 2026-11-05

- Complete flight-log exhibit: Government Exhibit 662-RR from *United States v. Maxwell* is now held as one complete, fingerprinted 118-page record.
- Agent package: the whole edition can be downloaded as a verified package (manifest format 3, which adds each entry's status and assessment) for research tools and AI agents.
- Intelligence topics: a new Intelligence heading gathers U.S. programs already in the Sourcebook, such as MHCHAOS and COINTELPRO, alongside the new published accounts below.
- New entries: 13 reference entries summarising published accounts of Israeli intelligence operations. They are marked as secondary-source summaries; allegations are attributed to their authors and are not independently established. In the main text, every statement drawn from a source carries a footnote; the only sentences there without one are lead-ins, pointers to other entries and our own notes on the limits of the record.
- Graded conclusions: every entry carries a status line; entries that state a conclusion of ours show how confident we are and what would change our view. See "How we grade conclusions".
- Credit to independent researchers: entries that build on peer projects say so.
- Sources checked against fuller holdings: for 15 of the sources this edition's entries cite, we now hold fingerprinted copies of the cited record, fuller than the excerpts or summaries we held before. They include House Oversight Production 01 (one 19-page document, DOJ-OGR-00015291 to 00015309, listed in the production is missing from our copy), the Maxwell trial, sentencing and appeal records, the 2020 Justice Department report on the Acosta-era case, the 2009 deposition of an Epstein flight-department employee (Volume I), and CourtListener's public copies of the federal dockets for the Maxwell case and the 2025 Florida grand-jury unsealing. Several are partial: for example, one book is held only as the publisher's preview, the appeal opinion as its first 8 of 26 pages, and the Florida docket as 7 of its 12 records.
- Corrected citations: six citations now describe their documents accurately. The Maxwell trial citation named a filing notice as the appeals decision; the federal prison records also include customs and airline records; the 2004–2005 contact book has printed entries with handwritten notes; the July 2025 interview of Ghislaine Maxwell is two dated transcripts; the House Oversight Democrats' release collection holds 42 releases, not 43; and the 2009 deposition's case number is 502008CA028051.
- Known gap in footnotes: our citation check covers an entry's main text. It does not cover headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, and those can state facts or describe people without a footnote. Every entry now carries a notice saying so. We are closing this gap in a later edition.
- Footnote display fix: five entries in the 2026-10 edition show raw footnote markers in place of working footnotes: Lolita Express, Limited Notification as Inoculation, Covert Action, Police Patrol vs. Fire Alarm Oversight and Security Commission Pattern. This is fixed from this edition. The 2026-10 edition stays as issued.
- Privacy policy changed: personal data in the record is now published, except for victims, survivors and minors, and medical information about private individuals the record does not name as taking part in the conduct described. See the redaction policy and the corrections and removal policy.
