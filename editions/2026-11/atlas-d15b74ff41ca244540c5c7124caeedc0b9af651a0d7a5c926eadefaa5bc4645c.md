# Department of Justice Office of the Inspector General Audit Authority

Edition label: `2026-11`

**Definition:** The statutory authority of the Department of Justice Office of the Inspector General (DOJ OIG), under the Inspector General Act of 1978 as recodified at 5 U.S.C. §§ 401–424, to conduct audits and investigations of DOJ programs and operations.[^5]

## Overview

DOJ OIG is the Department's principal audit and investigations office and has statutory independence,[^1] but 5 U.S.C. § 413 gives the Attorney General limited authority to control or prohibit work involving specified sensitive information, subject to written notice.[^6]

## Epstein Files Transparency Act compliance audit requests

Members of Congress asked DOJ OIG to audit the Department's compliance with the [Epstein Files Transparency Act](atlas-0446b9e969c5f0a9972f0039d70993cc3c8781967fcfb363675b709bb1b66c0f.md) as an independent check on implementation:[^2]

- **December 10, 2025** — Senators <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Schiff</span> and <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Durbin</span> sent a letter to Acting IG <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Don Berthiaume</span> requesting a chain-of-custody audit of all Epstein Files Transparency Act (EFTA)-mandated files.[^3]
- **December 24, 2025** — Twelve senators (lead <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Blumenthal</span>) sent a [broader audit request](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20251224lettertodojoigonepsteinreleasefinal.pdf) covering five substantive compliance dimensions (redaction protection of survivors, redaction not shielding abusers, withholding-only-for-statutory-reasons, narrow-tailoring, and politics-free decisionmaking).[^4]

On this page's reading, the two letters together test whether the OIG audit pathway will produce an institutional accountability record on EFTA compliance separate from any record DOJ leadership chooses to produce.

## Open Questions

- **Question:** Will Berthiaume formally accept either or both letters as triggering an audit, and on what timeline?
- **Question:** Does the OIG have the statutory authority to compel cooperation from a politically appointed AG who actively obstructs the audit?
- **Question:** Did Congress as a body (a committee or either chamber) ask DOJ OIG to audit EFTA compliance, as distinct from the two groups of individually signing senators who sent the December 10 and December 24, 2025 letters?
