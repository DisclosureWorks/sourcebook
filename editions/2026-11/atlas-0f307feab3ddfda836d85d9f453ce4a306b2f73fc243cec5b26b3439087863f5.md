# Black-Epstein Advisory Relationship

**Reference.** *Reports what the sources say. States no conclusion of ours.*

Edition label: `2026-11`

> Not everything on this page is footnoted. Our citation check covers the main text, but not headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, which may state facts without a footnote. Check the footnoted text or the source before relying on them.

**Definition:** The wiki's analytical handle for the ~$158M (per Dechert; Senator Wyden and the Bank of America complaint put it at $170M) financial relationship between <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Leon Black</span> (founder/CEO of <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Apollo Global Management</span>)[^17][^1] and <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Jeffrey Epstein</span> between 2012 and 2017[^2] — characterized in the [Dechert Apollo Investigation report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411494/000119312521016405/d118102dex991.htm) (January 2021)[^3] as payments for **tax and estate-planning advice**[^4], a characterization that has been actively contested in subsequent civil pleadings and congressional inquiries.[^5]

## Overview

The Black-Epstein advisory relationship is the wiki's reference financial relationship for understanding the **post-conviction-Epstein ultra-wealthy-client portfolio**: payments occurring during 2012–2017,[^6] *after* Epstein's 2007 SDFL non-prosecution agreement,[^7] *before* his July 2019 SDNY arrest.[^8] The $158M figure comes from the Dechert report's reconstruction.[^9]

## Two Layers of Contested Characterization

### Layer 1 — Dechert's "tax advice" framing (2021)

Per [Dechert LLP. “Investigation of Epstein/Black Relationship and Any Relationship Between Epstein and Apollo Global Management, Inc.” Memorandum to the Apollo Conflicts Committee, January 22, 2021. Filed as Exhibit 99.1 to Apollo Global Management, Inc., Form 8-K, January 25, 2021, SEC accession no. 0001193125-21-016405.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411494/000119312521016405/d118102dex991.htm), the report finds no evidence of involvement in Epstein's trafficking enterprise.[^10] Black announced his planned retirement as Apollo CEO on the day the report was released.[^11]

### Layer 2 — Subsequent challenges (2023+)

The Dechert characterization has been challenged by:[^12]

- **[*Jane Doe v. Bank of America, N.A.* Class Action Complaint, No. 1:25-cv-08520-JSR (S.D.N.Y. filed October 15, 2025), ECF No. 1.](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.nysd.651138/gov.uscourts.nysd.651138.1.0.pdf)** — civil pleading treating the "tax advice" characterization as contested[^13]
- **[Wyden, Ron. Letter to Pam Bondi, Attorney General; Scott Bessent, Secretary of the Treasury; and Kashyap Patel, Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation. U.S. Senate Committee on Finance, June 16, 2025.](https://www.finance.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/wyden_doj_treasury_epstein_letter.pdf)**

## How It Appears in This Research

In this research, the relationship appears in three settings:

1. **[Suspected Epstein Financial Offenses](atlas-030885beb2bad9dcb8e273d9251f7c1aa7d3bbc0f7911080016499eec7ea3714.md)** — post-NPA financial-counterparty relationship; pre-arrest period[^14]
2. **Civil-litigation track** — referenced in BofA-Epstein pleadings[^15]
3. **Congressional-inquiry track** — referenced in Senator Wyden's June 16, 2025 letter resubmitting his March 11, 2025 document request on payments to Epstein "by several ultra-wealthy individuals, including billionaire financier Leon Black"[^18]

## Open Questions

- **Question:** Which portions, if any, of the Black-Epstein payments funded settlements, legal-defense disbursements, or other activities beyond the services Dechert described?
- **Question:** Did the Dechert report itself draw any causal link between its findings and Black's same-day retirement announcement?
- **Question:** Does Senator Wyden's June 16, 2025 DOJ-Treasury letter identify the Black-Epstein advisory relationship as *the* subject of its investigation, and does it treat the "tax advice" characterization as the specific contested premise being tested?
- **Question:** Is the Black-Epstein advisory relationship the explicitly named subject of Wyden's 2025 DOJ-Treasury letter, or one of several named examples within it?
- **Question:** Does any source rank the Black-Epstein relationship as "major" among Epstein's post-NPA financial counterparties?
- **Question:** Given the USVI second amended complaint’s allegation that Southern Trust funded the Epstein Enterprise,[^16] which transactions connect Black’s advisory payments to the uses alleged in that litigation?
- **Question:** What independent evidence corroborates or challenges Dechert’s characterization of the services for which Black paid Epstein?
