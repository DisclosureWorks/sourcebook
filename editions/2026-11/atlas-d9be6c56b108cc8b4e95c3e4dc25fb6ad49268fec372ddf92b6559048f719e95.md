# SDNY Epstein Investigation

**Reference.** *Reports what the sources say. States no conclusion of ours.*

Edition label: `2026-11`

> Not everything on this page is footnoted. Our citation check covers the main text, but not headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, which may state facts without a footnote. Check the footnoted text or the source before relying on them.

**Jurisdiction:** Southern District of New York[^1]  
**Lead:** U.S. Attorney <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Geoff Berman</span>[^2]  
**Key actions:** <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Epstein</span> indictment (July 2019)[^3]; Maxwell indictment (June 2020)[^4]; Maxwell conviction (2021)[^5]

## Overview

Barr recalled Justice Department discussions about making sure the 2019 charges did not run afoul of the NPA.[^6]

## AG Barr's Role

<span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">William Barr</span> testified that he did not delve into the Epstein investigation and left it to the investigators:[^7]

- Does not recall reviewing the indictment; may have been shown it[^8]
- Did not approve charge decisions; does not recall approving subpoenas, though notes it's possible issues came up[^9]
- Did not recall communicating directly with any SDNY line prosecutors[^10]
- Barr recalled <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">David Bowdich</span> (FBI Deputy Director) as his primary FBI contact; Barr said he thought SDNY communications would have gone more to the Deputy Attorney General's office[^11]
- Believed he was told the substance of Maxwell's case when prosecutors were ready to indict, but did not review the evidence himself[^12]

Barr first learned of the investigation around the time of Epstein's arrest (July 2019), possibly slightly before.[^13]

## Investigation Scope (Barr's Account)

Barr states he was never briefed that SDNY had actionable evidence against any of the prominently named figures:[^14]

- <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Trump</span>, Clinton, Richardson, <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Mitchell</span>, <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Dubin</span>, Gates, <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Black</span>, <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Dershowitz</span>, Branson[^15]

Barr testified that he could not recall whether SDNY filed urgent reports beyond Epstein and Maxwell;[^16] he said a report "quite possibly" existed for Prince Andrew and that one should have been considered given the sensitivity.[^17]

Barr testified it was possible that <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Geoff Berman</span>'s SDNY had not told him how deep it was in the investigation of particular individuals[^18] — Barr describes the SDNY as "relatively well-known for playing its cards very close to the vest."[^19]

## Witness Cooperation

SDNY sought <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Prince Andrew</span> as a witness;[^20] Barr testified that he was aware of a dispute over Andrew's cooperation.[^21] <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Geoffrey Berman</span> made public remarks about the dispute outside Epstein's Manhattan residence on January 27, 2020, as reported at the time by the Associated Press[^29]; Barr criticized the handling.[^22]

## Post-Epstein Death

The investigation continued after Epstein's death, resulting in Maxwell's indictment.[^23] Whether any further investigation continued after Maxwell's conviction is unclear from Barr's testimony.[^24]

## Barr on the SDNY

- Barr argues SDNY's leak culture would prevent cover-ups;[^25] he also describes the SDNY as playing its cards very close to the vest[^26] and says it may not have fully briefed him.[^27]
- Barr never directly asked Berman which prominent figures were being investigated.[^28]

## Open Questions

- Was the SDNY investigation the primary federal prosecution of <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Jeffrey Epstein</span> and <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Ghislaine Maxwell</span>, as opposed to one of several concurrent federal proceedings?
- Did the SDNY investigation run in parallel with the earlier Florida [Non-Prosecution Agreement (Florida NPA)](atlas-27a5255e75c8b7189218433d8640b0342bd1ed7114879ef40fa55ef4f56006a8.md), rather than only beginning after it concluded?
