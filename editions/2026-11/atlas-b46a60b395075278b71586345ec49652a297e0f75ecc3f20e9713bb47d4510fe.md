# Victoria's Secret Recruitment Allegations

**Reference.** *Reports what the sources say. States no conclusion of ours.*

Edition label: `2026-11`

> Not everything on this page is footnoted. Our citation check covers the main text, but not headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, which may state facts without a footnote. Check the footnoted text or the source before relying on them.

**Definition:** Whitney Webb reports allegations that <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Epstein</span> used a claimed Victoria's Secret connection as a lure[^1] and that <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Ghislaine Maxwell</span> attended company events.[^2] Webb relays media descriptions of an "Epstein-Victoria's Secret pimp pipeline" and calls young models lured through Victoria's Secret "a key target" for recruitment, but she does not describe a coordinated recruitment filter run through the branded modeling circuit, and she does not place MC2 or <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Jean-Luc Brunel</span> at Victoria's Secret events.[^3]

## Overview

<span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Jeffrey Epstein</span> managed the personal finances of Leslie Wexner (founder of L Brands, who acquired Victoria's Secret in 1982) and, according to Webb, became increasingly involved in the affairs of the Wexner-owned lingerie brand.[^4] Whitney Webb (*One Nation Under Blackmail*, ch.[^5] 18) argues that Epstein exploited his Wexner/Victoria's Secret association as a lure;[^6] the cited account does not establish that Victoria's Secret as a company was an active operational component of his trafficking network.

Webb describes Epstein posing as a Victoria's Secret scout or owner and using the brand as a lure.[^7] Webb's account, and its limits, are:[^7]
1. Webb discusses Victoria's Secret events and, separately, Brunel and MC2;[^13] the cited material does not document their operating together at those events.
2. Webb reports that some aspiring models were introduced to Epstein by booking agents or friends, and that he presented himself as a Victoria's Secret scout or even its "owner".[^8]

## Leslie Wexner Connection

Epstein held power of attorney over Wexner's personal finances (granted in 1991, according to AP reporting), which let him make investments, do business deals, buy property and help develop the New Albany estate; Wexner says Epstein stepped back from managing those finances in 2007.[^9] The nature of Wexner's knowledge of and participation in Epstein's conduct is disputed.[^10] Wexner has said he was "deceived" by Epstein and that he severed ties in 2007, before Epstein's June 2008 state plea; Justice Department records reported by AP show Wexner emailed Epstein on June 26, 2008, and Wexner's spokesperson says the 2007 date refers to firing Epstein as financial adviser and revoking his power of attorney.[^11]

Whitney Webb's analysis treats the VS pipeline not as something Wexner necessarily designed or controlled, but as something Epstein exploited as a lure through the access Wexner's business empire provided.[^12]

## Open Questions

- Did Wexner give Epstein the Manhattan townhouse, and what records establish the Beneficial Ownership chain and terms of any transfer?

- Was the VS pipeline documented in any civil litigation discovery, or is Whitney Webb's account based primarily on victim testimony and circumstantial evidence?
- Did HOGR examine the Wexner-Epstein relationship and the VS connection?
- What was the scope of Epstein's power of attorney over Wexner, and when exactly was it executed and revoked?
- Did Epstein have access to L Brands' business operations beyond managing Wexner's personal finances?
- Did Victoria's Secret run casting calls or other events, beyond its runway fashion show, that brought large numbers of aspiring models into contact with VS representatives?
- Is there a primary source documenting Jean-Luc Brunel or MC2 operating jointly with Victoria's Secret events, beyond Webb's separate discussion of each?
- Did Epstein's role in Wexner's affairs extend beyond personal finances and property to broader 'personal arrangements'?
