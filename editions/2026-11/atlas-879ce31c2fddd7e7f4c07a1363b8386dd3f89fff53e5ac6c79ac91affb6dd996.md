# Edge Foundation

**Definition:** The Edge Foundation is a nonprofit intellectual salon[^17] founded by literary agent John Brockman that organized annual gatherings of scientists, technologists, and other thinkers.[^1] Whitney Webb reports that <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Epstein</span> supplied $638,000 of the $857,000 in donations Edge raised from 2001 through 2017,[^2] about 74%.[^3]

## Overview

Edge Foundation hosted the annual "Edge Annual Dinner" and maintained the website edge.org,[^4] which published responses to annual big-questions.[^16] The Edge community included <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Nathan Myhrvold</span>, Linda Stone, <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Joi Ito</span>, Esther Dyson, and <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Bill Gates</span>.[^5]

Whitney Webb (*One Nation Under Blackmail*, Ch. 20) reports that Epstein supplied $638,000 of the $857,000 in donations Edge raised from 2001 through 2017,[^6] about 74%.[^7] Webb describes Epstein's participation in Edge and characterizes it as providing access and cover for cultivating prominent figures:[^8][^9]
1. **Social access:** The social world of elite scientists.[^8]
2. **Recruitment:** Cover and access to target and cultivate prominent figures in Silicon Valley and academia.[^9]

## Epstein's Eugenics Interest

Epstein had a documented interest in eugenics[^10] and genetic manipulation.[^11] He reportedly expressed interest in using his New Mexico ranch[^12] to impregnate women with his sperm to seed the human race.[^13] Whitney Webb links this interest to his technology ties: she writes that Epstein's interest in technology 'appears to have been informed by his obsessions with genetics and eugenics,' and that he targeted Big Tech leaders 'via Edge and other means' to gain access to the data they controlled.[^15]

## Aftermath

Webb reports that Epstein stopped donating in 2015, the same year Edge discontinued its annual Billionaires' Dinner tradition.[^14]
