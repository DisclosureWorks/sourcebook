# MCC Death Investigation

**Reference.** *Reports what the sources say. States no conclusion of ours.*

Edition label: `2026-11`

> Not everything on this page is footnoted. Our citation check covers the main text, but not headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, which may state facts without a footnote. Check the footnoted text or the source before relying on them.

**Event:** Death of <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Jeffrey Epstein</span> at Metropolitan Correctional Center (MCC), New York[^1]  
**Date:** August 10, 2019[^2]  
**Official ruling:** Suicide by hanging[^3] (Material Handler Thomas, who found him, described him suspended from the top bunk in a near-seated position)[^4]  
**Investigations:** The New York City Chief Medical Examiner ruled the death a suicide by hanging;[^5] the FBI found no criminality associated with the death;[^6] DOJ OIG found no evidence contradicting the FBI's determination.[^7]

## Timeline

- **July 6, 2019:** Arrested;[^8] assigned to SHU[^9] (9 South, 9th floor)[^10] July 7 due to notoriety[^11]
- **July 9:** Formal psychological assessment; denied suicidal history; no diagnosis[^12]
- **July 23, 2019, 1:27 AM:** CO found <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Epstein</span> with orange cloth around neck — cellmate said he tried to hang himself.[^13] Medical staff: friction marks on neck and knee.[^14] Placed on suicide watch.[^15] BOP administrative record: **Code 228 (SELF-MUTILATION)** disciplinary charge filed;[^16] Epstein placed in Administrative Detention July 29 pending DHO hearing.[^17] Charges ultimately NOT SUSTAINED (insufficient evidence).[^18] Epstein gave conflicting accounts: (1) cellmate tried to kill him; (2) he didn't know what happened; he later asked to be re-housed with the same cellmate.[^19]
- **July 24:** Removed from suicide watch; placed on psychological observation[^20]
- **July 30:** Psychological observation ended;[^21] **Psychology Dept emailed 70+ MCC staff** requiring Epstein be assigned appropriate cellmate.[^22] Warden selected new cellmate in consultation with BOP executive leadership; that cellmate remained until August 9.[^23]
- **August 8:** USMS sent two emails to numerous MCC staff notifying them cellmate would transfer August 9 — no action taken.[^24] Epstein met with attorneys and signed new **Last Will and Testament** (MCC staff unaware until after death).[^25]
- **August 9:** Cellmate transferred; no replacement assigned — in direct violation of the Psychology directive[^26] which supervisors told OIG they had communicated to all SHU staff, though several SHU staff said they did not know of it. Second Circuit unsealed ~2,000 pages of Maxwell civil documents;[^27] widespread media coverage.[^28]
- **August 9 (6:58–7:19 p.m.):**[^29] Supervisor allowed Epstein to make **unrecorded, unmonitored phone call** in violation of BOP policy.[^30] Epstein said he was calling his mother; he actually called a personal contact.[^31] Identity not disclosed.[^32]
- **4 PM, August 9:** The count was recorded inaccurately; staff did not physically count inmates.[^33]
- **~8 PM, August 9:** All SHU inmates locked in cells; Epstein confirmed alone in cell.[^34]
- **~10:40 PM, August 9:** Last observed approach to the L Tier before breakfast.[^35] No rounds or counts conducted after this time.[^36] **Count slips and round sheets were falsified** to show compliance.[^37] OIG found that an unnamed Material Handler worked 24 consecutive hours (3 shifts);[^38] the unnamed Material Handler told OIG that no one did the 10 p.m. count because they were tired.[^39]
- **August 8, 2019:** DVR failure for most SHU cameras began July 29;[^40] MCC discovered on August 8 but did not repair before Epstein's death.[^41]
- **August 10, ~6:30 AM:** CO <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Tova Noel</span> and Material Handler <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Michael Thomas</span> began breakfast delivery.[^42] Thomas found Epstein not responding — unlocked cell;[^43] found Epstein hanging from top of bunkbed with orange string, near-seated position, buttocks ~1–1.5 inches off floor.[^44] Thomas ripped string, lowered Epstein, performed CPR.[^45] Outside medical personnel arrived; Epstein transported to hospital and pronounced dead.[^46]
- **August 10:** Barr recalled a morning call from his chief of staff, <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Brian Rabbitt</span>, reporting an apparent suicide (Barr placed the call on a Sunday; August 10, 2019 was a Saturday).[^47] A supervisory staff attorney forwarded Warden N'Diaye's official notification at 1:57 p.m.[^48]
- **August 10 (same day):** <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Barr</span> directed IG <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Michael Horowitz</span> to dispatch investigators;[^49] Barr later personally reviewed MCC security footage.[^50]
- **August 11:** NYC Chief Medical Examiner autopsy:[^51] **cause — hanging; manner — suicide**.[^52] Toxicology negative.[^53] Multiple nooses were found in the cell on August 10.[^54] A family preservation request was sent to MCC seeking records held by the named agencies.[^55]
- **Within one week:** Barr recalled replacing the BOP director and deputy within about a week[^56]; DOJ announced their appointments August 19, 2019.[^130] Barr recalled stopping MCC intake and believed he redirected prisoners to Brooklyn MDC; he did not date that action.[^57]
- **August 27:** U.S. District Judge Richard M. Berman held a status hearing;[^58] defense asked the court to oversee the death investigation;[^59] the government opposed; victims spoke for 2.5 hours.[^60]
- **June 2023:** DOJ OIG Report 23-085 published.[^61]

## Physical Evidence Supporting Suicide

From OIG Report 23-085 unless the item says otherwise; Barr's deposition overlaps on several points:[^138]

- Medical Examiner: injuries more consistent with, and indicative of, a suicide by hanging rather than a homicide by strangulation;[^62] no defensive wounds;[^63] no marks on hands; no broken fingernails; no debris under nails; no knuckle contusions; no body bruising (one arm abrasion consistent with convulsing from hanging)[^64]
- Toxicology negative for medications and illegal substances[^65]
- Prior incident July 23 (contested — Code 228 charges not sustained);[^66] placed on suicide watch[^67]
- Signed new Last Will and Testament August 8 (2 days before death)[^68]
- Unmonitored phone call to personal contact August 9[^69] (Barr recalled a call to someone he believed was a girlfriend in Byelorussia and interpreted it as consistent with saying goodbye[^70] — OIG: personal contact, identity not disclosed)[^71]
- Cellmate transferred; Epstein alone; excess linens including multiple nooses in cell[^72]
- Failed bail;[^73] Maxwell documents unsealed August 9[^74] with extensive media coverage[^75]
- Three interviewed inmates with sight of Epstein’s cell door told OIG nobody entered or exited that cell after his return.[^76]

## The "Perfect Storm of Screwups"

A phrase the questioner attributed to Barr, which he accepted, for six decisions or failures preceding or occurring that night:[^139]
1. Removed from suicide watch[^77]
2. No cellmate assigned[^78]
3. 30-minute checks not performed[^79]
4. Guards falsified logs[^80]
5. DVR not recording (see [DVR Failure (MCC Tier L Camera)](atlas-c6c36087a82eb0661e908f8fa2fb324a23b863415b576818a06c3bec3c3432d1.md))[^81]
6. Institutional counts not done[^82]

## Homicide Theory — Dr. Michael Baden

Medical examiner Dr. Michael Baden said the autopsy findings were more consistent with homicidal strangulation than hanging.[^129] Barr recalled no one expressing agreement with Baden during his conversations.[^83] OIG found nothing contradicting the FBI’s no-criminality finding; Barr was not aware of an investigation concluding homicide.[^84]

## Physical Access — SHU

Barr argued that undetected access up the stairway was not physically possible:[^85]

- SHU access requires remote central control door + key held by officers[^86]
- Two eyewitness inmates on the same tier saw no one enter[^87]
- "this would've required coordination from probably two dozen people maybe within the prison"[^88]

## FBI Independent Footage Review (July 2025)

The joint DOJ/FBI memorandum, labeled July 2025 in its PDF metadata,[^136] announced an independent FBI review of SHU footage:[^89]

- FBI enhanced the footage (contrast, color, sharpness) and posted raw and enhanced versions to the DOJ website.[^90]
- Confirmed: from ~10:40 PM August 9, 2019, to ~6:30 AM August 10, **"nobody entered any of the tiers in the SHU."**[^91]
- This claim is consistent with Barr's account but comes from FBI's own independent review, not Barr's briefing.[^92]
- The memorandum separately announces no further disclosure and cites footage in support of its suicide conclusion.[^93]

## Contested Footage

CBS reported the following observations from the released footage and its experts’ analysis:[^132]

- An "orange shape" on the stairs[^133]
- A possible third unidentified individual[^134]

Asked about the orange shape and a possible third person, Barr said he did not recall focusing on an orange shape[^94] and saw nothing of an unidentified person in the footage he reviewed.[^95] The camera directly covering Tier L was live-streaming but not recording due to [DVR Failure (MCC Tier L Camera)](atlas-c6c36087a82eb0661e908f8fa2fb324a23b863415b576818a06c3bec3c3432d1.md).[^96]

**Two additional cameras** (elevator bank,[^97] 10 South Officers' Station (adjacent unit; no view of the SHU)) were recording[^98].

## OIG Report Key Findings (23-085, June 2023)

The OIG identified numerous and serious failures, including:[^99]

1. **Cellmate requirement not met:** Psychology Dept's July 30 requirement[^100] was emailed to over 70 staff and, according to supervisors, communicated to SHU staff, though several SHU staff told OIG they did not know of it; USMS notified MCC August 8 the cellmate was leaving; no action.[^101]
2. **Records falsified:** Count slips and round sheets falsified.[^102] Multiple employees created false documentation;[^103] USAO SDNY declined prosecution of other employees for false records on the day before and the day of the death.[^104]
3. **Criminal prosecution → dismissal:** CO Tova Noel and Material Handler Michael Thomas charged federally for falsifying records — charges **dismissed** after successful deferred prosecution agreements.[^105]
4. **Camera failure:** DVR system failed July 29;[^106] MCC discovered August 8; not repaired before the death.[^107] FBI forensic analysis found 3 faulty hard drives; system never successfully reassembled.[^108]
5. **Staffing:** OIG found that an unnamed Material Handler worked 24 consecutive hours;[^109] the unnamed Material Handler told OIG the 10 p.m. count was skipped because they were tired.[^110]
6. **Cell security failure:** Excess linens in cell;[^111] records did not show when the cell was last searched;[^112] multiple nooses constructed and not discovered.[^113]

The OIG found **no evidence contradicting the FBI's determination** that no criminality was associated with the death.[^114]

## Aftermath

Guards <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Tova Noel</span> and <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">Michael Thomas</span> charged with falsifying records; charges dismissed via DPA.[^135] Other employees who falsified records on the day before and the day of the death were not prosecuted.[^115] Barr recalled being informed before action and said he was not involved in prosecutorial decisions.[^116] Barr recalled quickly reassigning the warden[^117]; the new BOP director was announced August 19, 2019.[^131] Eight OIG recommendations[^118] — all accepted by BOP.[^119]

## Open Questions

- Was DNA testing done on cell bedsheets?
- Which MCC recordings other than the released SHU common-area video remain unreleased, and what do the elevator-bank and 10 South Officers’ Station recordings show about activity elsewhere that night?[^123]
- Who did Epstein actually call on August 9 (OIG says personal contact, not his mother)?
- Do the IG and FBI reports fully reconcile the Baden hyoid findings?
- Was Epstein's body found in a ‘kneeling’ position, as a congressional questioner characterized the chief medical examiner's original finding, or in the near-seated position OIG and the Material Handler who found him described?
- Did Warden N'Diaye's August 10, 2019 death notification (forwarded by a supervisory staff attorney at 1:57 p.m.) reach Epstein's family directly, or only defense counsel and DOJ personnel?
- On what date did MCC New York actually stop new inmate intake and redirect admissions to Brooklyn MDC, given that Barr's recollection of this action was hedged and undated?
- How did the qualifications and shift assignments of the staff working in the SHU on August 9–10, 2019, affect their performance of required checks?
- Is the OIG report the most detailed primary source on Epstein's death?
- Was the July 29 DVR failure (one day before Epstein returned to SHU from psychological observation (BOP's SENTRY database wrongly recorded the return as July 29)) coincidental?
- Why were charges against other employees who falsified records on the day before and the day of Epstein’s death declined?
