# OCDETF Operation Chain Reaction

**Reference.** *Reports what the sources say. States no conclusion of ours.*

Edition label: `2026-11`

> Not everything on this page is footnoted. Our citation check covers the main text, but not headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, which may state facts without a footnote. Check the footnoted text or the source before relying on them.

**Definition:** The Drug Enforcement Administration's Organized Crime Drug Enforcement Task Forces investigation of Jeffrey Epstein and 14 other targets, whom Wyden calls co-conspirators,[^1] (case number NY-NYS-0829),[^2] documented in a May 18, 2015 OCDETF Fusion Center memorandum (OFC-TP-15-12392 / SODOFC-15-12392; DOJ Bates EFTA00173953)[^3] for "illegitimate wire transfers" tied to "illicit drug and/or prostitution activities occurring in the U.S. Virgin Islands and New York City."[^4] No drug-trafficking or money-laundering charges against the fifteen named targets are identified in Wyden's letter;[^5] the operation's conclusion date and final result remained questions posed to DEA.[^6]

## Overview

 It is described in Senator Ron Wyden's [February 25, 2026 letter to DEA Administrator Terrance Cole](https://www.finance.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/letter_from_senator_wyden_to_dea_2-25-26.pdf), which cites the underlying OCDETF memorandum (Bates EFTA00173953, released via [EFTA](atlas-0446b9e969c5f0a9972f0039d70993cc3c8781967fcfb363675b709bb1b66c0f.md) Data Set 9, heavily redacted).[^7] The memo documents[^28][^29]:

- **14 other targets in addition to Epstein** (Wyden calls them co-conspirators) — identities redacted in the EFTA release[^8]
- **Criminal predicate:** "illegitimate wire transfers" tied to "illicit drug and/or prostitution activities"[^9]
- **Geographic scope:** USVI and NYC[^10]
- **Data compilation:** suspicious financial transactions, border crossings, Treasury Department data, and data from "a range of other federal law enforcement agencies"[^11]
- **Document classification:** "sensitive but unclassified"[^12]

Wyden's letter documents that (1) the investigation existed and reached a scope sufficient to warrant an OCDETF Fusion Center memo, (2) the letter identifies no drug-trafficking or money-laundering charges against Epstein or the 14 other targets and asks the DEA why none were filed,[^13] and (3) [OCDETF](atlas-2a344adb9a20f49e51805f89cc3d118e47408e53e0804de6f9543bd675f00c90.md) was dismantled by the Trump administration.[^14]

Wyden alleges — but does not prove — that "the DEA and DOJ during the first Trump Administration moved to terminate this investigation in order to protect pedophiles."[^15]

## Analytical Significance

Other investigative and financial-compliance tracks in the record, each cited in its own item, include:

1. SDFL federal sex-offense investigation resolved by non-prosecution agreement, with state prosecution in Palm Beach County (→ [Non-Prosecution Agreement (Florida NPA)](atlas-27a5255e75c8b7189218433d8640b0342bd1ed7114879ef40fa55ef4f56006a8.md))[^16]
2. FBI Los Angeles October 2020 informant memo[^17] (→ <span class="link-unavailable" title="Not published in this edition">FBI/intelligence-allegation record</span>)
3. Treasury/bank-compliance SAR investigations ($1.5B four-bank aggregate documented in [Raskin, Jamie. Letter to Robin Vince, Chief Executive Officer, Bank of New York Mellon. U.S. House Committee on the Judiciary, October 8, 2025.](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2025-10-08.raskin-to-vince-bny-mellon-re-epstein.pdf))[^18]
4. What Wyden describes as indicators of a Russian-intelligence relationship[^25] (Belyakov/FSB email channel[^19] + 15 meetings with Russian government officials and executives of Russian state-owned banks 2015–2017[^20], per [Wyden, Ron. Letter to Leon D. Black. U.S. Senate Committee on Finance, March 20, 2026 (redacted).](https://www.finance.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/senator_wyden_letter_to_leon_black_redacted.pdf))

The OCDETF memorandum does not establish why, as Wyden's letter puts it, no federal drug-trafficking or money-laundering charges were ever filed,[^13] that any subject received institutional protection, or that Epstein's enterprise was formally classified as transnational organized crime.

## Open Questions

- **What does the unredacted EFTA00173953 memo contain?** Who are the 14 other targets?
- **When did Chain Reaction conclude, and with what result, as Wyden asks in Question 4?**[^21]
- **Did Chain Reaction generate any referral to other federal investigations through the federal partnerships Wyden describes?**[^22]
- **Does Chain Reaction bear on the Russian channel described in Wyden's March 2026 letter, including the Belyakov FSB-academy email channel and 15 meetings with Russian government officials and executives of Russian state-owned banks 2015–2017?**[^24] What, if any, connection goes beyond the May 2015 memorandum's overlap with the beginning of that period?[^23]
- **Was there a documented 2019 FBI re-opening of an Epstein investigation?**
- **Was there an earlier federal drug-enforcement investigation of Epstein than the DEA New York case C1-11-0049, listed in the May 2015 OCDETF memo as opened on December 17, 2010 with the status "Pending Judicial"?**[^28]
- **How does Chain Reaction compare with the other investigative and financial-compliance tracks listed above, and what would establish its significance as an additional investigative vector?**
- **What legal basis does DOJ state for the EFTA00173953 redactions?**
- **Which institutions filed the seven suspicious-activity reports on Epstein listed in the May 2015 memo, and what action followed the reporting the memo found in FinCEN's Gateway database?**[^29] How do those reports relate to the four-bank reporting to Treasury described in the Raskin letter?[^26]
- **Beyond the Belyakov background and contacts that Wyden describes as indicators, what would establish whether Epstein had a Russian-intelligence channel?**[^27]
