# Lolita Express

**Reference.** *Reports what the sources say. States no conclusion of ours.*

Edition label: `2026-11`

> Not everything on this page is footnoted. Our citation check covers the main text, but not headings, the lists of related entries and sources, or open questions, which may state facts without a footnote. Check the footnoted text or the source before relying on them.

**Definition:** Popular media nickname[^1] used in press coverage of Jeffrey Epstein's air travel. This page documents Epstein's Boeing 727 (FAA tail number N908JE, per pilot-logbook records)[^2], a large-cabin aircraft.[^3]

## Overview

Epstein bought the 727 from Leslie Wexner's company, The Limited,[^4] which replaced it with a Boeing Business Jet (BBJ);[^5] the 727 stayed in Epstein's service until about 2016 and was sold in 2018,[^6] per Morrison's 2009 sworn testimony and the chief pilot's 2020 FBI interview.

Rodgers told the FBI he did not know how the term "Lolita Express" originated and disputed a press account about it;[^7] Morrison referred to the aircraft as "his 727."[^8]

The aircraft sits inside the broader [Epstein flight department (JEGE)](atlas-99b093eadc82503e84cb6354f66a06dcdcbaf266ab8f8f7bd1a3721416185d45.md) operation; its maintenance and crew were drawn from the same staff that handled the Gulfstreams.[^9] Surviving passenger evidence for the 727 comes from the Maxwell-trial pilot flight logbooks (Government Exhibit 662-RR)[^10] and from witness depositions.[^11]

## Documentary Record

- **Passenger-record exhibit.** The 727's pilot logs appear in [Government Exhibit 662-RR](https://oversight.house.gov/release/oversight-committee-releases-epstein-records-provided-by-the-department-of-justice/) alongside the Gulfstream logs; DOJ-OGR-00022263, image p. 91, records legs 75–76, dated March 19 and 21, 2002, involving Bill Clinton and Doug Band on N908JE.[^20]
- **Acquisition context.** Morrison's 2009 deposition documents that Epstein bought the 727 from Leslie Wexner when The Limited replaced it with a Boeing Business Jet; the Rodgers FBI interview dates Epstein's purchase to January 2001 and states that his 727 flew until about 2016 and was sold in 2018.[^12]
- The name "Lolita Express" appears in journalism;[^13] analysts should distinguish between (a) claims tied to specific N908JE pilot-log entries (verifiable primary evidence)[^14] and (b) generic "Lolita Express" references (for example, the press statement that Prince Andrew flew on it, which Rodgers said he had never made).[^15]
